## Design Context

### Users
Brand owners bidding for visible placements, and makers who want to fork Brand Anything to launch an auction around their own object or project. The default repository should be immediately understandable before the first bid arrives.

### Brand Personality
Open, credible, and quietly playful. The product should feel like a polished Apple-adjacent auction experience without pretending that demo traffic, sponsors, or revenue are real.

### Aesthetic Direction
Keep the existing light, restrained, Mac-inspired visual language. Favor a clean auction surface, legible empty states, and honest system feedback over decorative dashboards or fabricated social proof.

### Design Principles
- Start truthfully: an unused template has open inventory, zero bids, and no placeholder sponsors.
- Make the next action obvious: every empty location should explain its opening price and invite the first bid.
- Reveal composed visuals atomically: the Final Look appears only after the device and every active brand asset are ready.
- Keep ownership portable: project identity and calls to action point to the open-source Brand Anything template.
- Default to US dollars while keeping the optional euro view clearly secondary and indicative.
